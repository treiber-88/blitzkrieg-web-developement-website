Why is it called Blitzkrieg Web Development?

The founder chose Blitzkrieg Web Development because "blitzkrieg" is a really cool word that means lightning websites in German. Oh, wait. It means lightning war.

How much will I be paying for my website?

What happens is you will tell us what you want your website to look like and what you want it to do, and we will tell you how much the upfront pay is (how much you pay us for the website as soon as it is finished), and then how much the monthly maintenence fee will cost.

I just want a website and don't really care about what it looks like so that it is as cheap as possible. Do you do that sort of thing?

Absolutely. You just tell us your business name (or whatever you want the website for) and we will make you the cheapest website possible.

What's the Stoat server invite? What is Stoat?

Stoat is a lot like Discord, except it is way less well known and is less prone to bot attacks. It is more secure in general. The reason that we would really appreciate clients joining is because instead of going back and forth on email about changes that the client wants done to the website, we can just schedule a time to talk on Stoat. Then the client can have the website open as we make changes and tell us right away if they are satisfied or not.

I want somebody to make me an in-browser game. Do you do that?

Blitzkrieg does not really do that sort of thing. That is more videogame programming than a website.
